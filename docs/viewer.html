CBosses

Every boss in the plugin as it is built in game: block models with their own attacks, entrance and death. Drag to turn the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom. Pick a boss, then press Entrance, Death or any attack. Flying bosses flap, the egg really hatches, and deaths shatter into shards. The browser draws rough stand-ins for the block textures; in game every piece is a real Minecraft block.
